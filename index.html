The Quest for Unity

In the peaceful village of Ghalis, Galistan lives a quiet life with his beloved family — his sisters Mirka and Ashley, his wise mother Zelyra, and strong father Bjorn. Their days are filled with laughter and love. But one night, under a blood-red moon, everything changes. A dark force known as The Zluvs, led by the ruthless Zluv Tyrannos, invades the village. They steal crops, livestock… and Galistan’s entire family. Wounded and left behind, Galistan awakens to ruins and strange symbols carved into the earth — the only clue to his family’s fate. Now, with nothing left but vengeance and hope, Galistan begins his journey to rescue those he loves — and destroy those who took them.
